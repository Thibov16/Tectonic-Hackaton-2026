# Sick Leave Notification (Germany) - v1.0

1. The employee informs the employer on the first day of sickness before 09:00.
2. From the 4th calendar day of illness, a paper medical certificate (Arbeitsunfaehigkeitsbescheinigung) must be handed in to HR.
3. HR scans and stores the certificate in the personnel file within 2 working days.
