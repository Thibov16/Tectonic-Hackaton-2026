# Customer Payroll Data Handling - v3.1

1. Customer payroll data (names, salaries, bank details) is classified CONFIDENTIAL-HIGH.
2. It must never be sent by email, even internally; use the secure customer portal or approved encrypted transfer.
3. Exports to local machines are forbidden. Work inside the approved virtual desktop only.
4. Bank detail changes require dual approval: one payroll consultant and one team lead, plus a call-back to a known customer contact.
5. Suspected data leaks must be reported to the Security Desk within 1 hour.
