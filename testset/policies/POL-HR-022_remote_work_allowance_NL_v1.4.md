# Home Office Expense Compensation (Netherlands) - v1.4

1. Scope: Dutch employees with a hybrid working agreement.
2. Compensation: EUR 2.35 per day worked from home, paid per month in arrears.
3. Maximum: 214 days per calendar year.
4. Requires the employee to register home-working days in the HR portal.
