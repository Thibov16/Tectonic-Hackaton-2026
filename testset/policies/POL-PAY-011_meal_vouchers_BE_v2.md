# Electronic Meal Vouchers (Belgium) - v2.0

1. Maximum face value per worked day: EUR 8.00.
2. Minimum employee contribution: EUR 1.09 per voucher.
3. Maximum employer contribution: EUR 6.91 per voucher.
4. Vouchers are granted only for days actually worked on-site or remotely; not for days of leave or sickness.
5. Vouchers are loaded on the employee card by the 5th of the following month.
