# Remote Work Allowance (Belgium) - v2.1

1. Scope: All Belgian employees with a signed teleworking agreement.
2. Allowance: EUR 140.00 per month for structural teleworking of at least 2 days per week.
3. Employees teleworking occasionally (fewer than 2 days per week) receive EUR 0.
4. The allowance is paid with the monthly salary, exempt from social contributions within the legal ceiling.
5. Claims are automatic; no receipts required.
