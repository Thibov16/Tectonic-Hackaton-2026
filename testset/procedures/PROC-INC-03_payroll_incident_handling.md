# Payroll Incident Handling

Severity 1 (wrong net pay for more than 50 employees): notify Team Lead within 30 min, customer within 2 h, fix before next payment file is sent.
Severity 2 (wrong net pay for 1-50 employees): notify Team Lead same day, correction in next pay run or urgent correction.
Severity 3 (cosmetic, e.g. payslip layout): log ticket, fix in next release.
Every Severity 1 and 2 incident requires a post-incident analysis within 10 working days.
