# Retroactive correction of company car benefit (BE)

1. Open the employee file in the payroll tool and go to Benefits > Company Car.
2. Change the start date of the benefit.
3. Recalculate the affected payroll periods.
4. Check the result against the payroll control report.

Known limitation: none.
