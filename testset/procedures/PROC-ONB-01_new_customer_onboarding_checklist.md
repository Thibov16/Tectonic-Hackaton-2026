# New Customer Onboarding Checklist

[ ] 1. Signed contract and DPA received
[ ] 2. Customer-specific deadlines entered in the Customer Parameters sheet
[ ] 3. Legal entity, joint committee (paritair comite) and payment calendar configured
[ ] 4. Test payroll run with 5 sample employees, approved by customer
[ ] 5. Named customer contacts registered for call-back verification (see POL-SEC-002, rule 4)
[ ] 6. Kick-off meeting held and minutes shared
