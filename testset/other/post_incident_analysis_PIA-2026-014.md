# PIA-2026-014: Wrong remote allowance for 14 employees (Random Customer BE)

Root cause: A consultant used the superseded v2.1 amount (EUR 140) taken from a cached copy on a personal drive.
Impact: 14 employees underpaid by EUR 11 per month for 10 months.
Actions: back pay executed; old policy copies removed; add the 'current version' banner to the intranet policy page.
Lesson: people trust the first document they find; nothing on the old file said it was superseded.
