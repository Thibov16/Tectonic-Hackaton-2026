# Remote Work Allowance (Belgium) - v3.0

1. Scope: All Belgian employees with a signed teleworking agreement.
2. Allowance: EUR 181.00 per month for structural teleworking of at least 2 days per week.
3. Occasional teleworking (1 day per week): EUR 75.00 per month.
4. Paid with the monthly salary, exempt from social contributions within the legal ceiling.
